# Project instructions

At the beginning of each development session, read `MASTER_DEVELOPMENT_DIRECTIVE.md` and `MOVEMENT_PHYSICS_DIRECTIVE.md` before changing the project. The first file is the shared source for artistic approval, commercial licensing, and multiplayer-first priorities. The second supplements it for movement, physics, and combat research. If they conflict, stop and ask. Claude Desktop and Cursor both follow those files.

This game may be sold, including on Steam. Treat it as a commercial product during development.

Read `GAME_DESIGN.md` for the game design, `BASELINE.md` for what has actually been built, and `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md` before adding any file that was not created for this project.

If the rights for a commercial game cannot be established, do not use the resource.

## Do not add unverified third-party material

Do not download, install, import, copy, or incorporate a third-party resource until its license and source have been verified and recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md`.

This covers models, clothing, weapons, animations, rigs, textures, materials, shaders, music, sound effects, ambience, scripts, libraries, Asset Store packages, mods, fonts, icons, UI, particles, and environments.

Copyright by itself is not a reason to reject a resource. What matters is whether the rights holder has allowed this commercial use.

## Preference order

1. Original work made for this game.
2. CC0 from an identifiable creator, after checking for trademarks, patents, privacy, publicity, and other rights that CC0 does not remove.
3. Open-source code under a verified permissive license such as MIT, BSD, or Apache-2.0, with its notices kept. A code license does not cover bundled art or audio.

The resource must be free to obtain and usable in a sold game. Do not add a paid, royalty, subscription, or commercial-upgrade dependency.

## Do not use

- Noncommercial, personal-use-only, or educational-use-only licenses.
- A missing or unclear license, or ownership that cannot be checked.
- Ripped or extracted commercial-game assets.
- Franchise characters, distinctive designs, or logos used without permission.
- Unauthorized music samples.
- Mods without explicit permission for commercial reuse.
- A license that blocks distribution inside a proprietary game.

Credit does not replace permission. Editing a file does not remove its license.

## Review before import

Record the creator, the original source, the exact license and version, commercial use, modification, distribution inside a sold game, attribution, redistribution limits, third-party rights, bundled dependencies, and any payment or ongoing duty. If a critical item is missing, reject the resource until it is resolved.

## Register

`THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md` is the register. Each entry is APPROVED, REVIEW REQUIRED, or REJECTED. Do not put a REVIEW REQUIRED or REJECTED resource in a commercial build. Do not delete an existing project file without the owner's approval. Flag it and propose a replacement.

Do not claim a resource is lawsuit-proof or public domain without evidence. A GitHub or store listing is not a license review. Unresolved legal questions need a qualified attorney before release, including the game name, major characters, and this register.

The original characters, world, and visual identity belong to this project. Do not add franchise assets. Do not add an assistant authorship signature. Keep legally required notices.

When in doubt, do not use the asset.
