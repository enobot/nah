# World timeline

## CANON

There is no approved calendar. The only established order is:

1. The champions already have lives, enemies, and beliefs before they arrive.
2. They wake in the Nexus and are told to fight.
3. The first game's adventure is a sequence of those fights and the clues around them.
4. The game ends with a conclusion the player earned, while the largest questions can remain open.

## PROPOSED

`STORY_TIMELINE.md` is one possible history: a grain treaty, a stolen crown, a burned pass, a frozen harbor, a fungal memory, and a banished fool. It depends on boss names that are not approved. Do not treat that file as the past of the universe.

If those names are approved later, that file becomes the order of events before the summons. Until then, writers should not add facts that contradict it without writing the change down here.

## MYSTERY

- How long the Nexus has existed.
- Whether this tournament is the first.
- What the realms call the same sky, if they share one.

## FUTURE

A later game may show one pre-summons event in person, such as the day a road burned or a harbor froze. That game has to match whatever version of the past is CANON by then. It is not scheduled.
