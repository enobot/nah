# Champion lore

## CANON

Every major champion needs a life outside the arena: a home, a want, a contradiction, and a reason the tournament matters to them. They do not all need to be rulers or gods. Ordinary people, criminals, and outcasts can matter more than a throne.

Boss behavior should match that life. A careful person studies. A reckless person overcommits. A trickster feints. An adaptive champion learns habits, with the limits already written in `GAME_DESIGN.md`.

Beating a boss can unlock a free reward tied to that person. Cosmetics do not change competitive stats. New gameplay rewards stay out of the standard competitive loadout.

The custom player does not need a unique novel for every species and class.

Every major champion has two stories. The owner set this on 2026-10-28.

- **The story everyone believes.** What the tournament, the crowd, and the early dialogue say about that champion.
- **The story that happened.** What a later visit to that champion's home can show.

The two may match. They may also describe the same act and disagree about what it meant. A twist here is a missing fact or a second meaning. It is not a rule that every champion is secretly kind or secretly cruel.

The first game can tell the believed story and leave a crack: a line, a scar, a reward, a note. The home story can wait. Do not dump the second story in an exposition scene.

### The flower sisters

Five queens, one family. Working realm: the Five Gardens. They are an original people. They are not fairies or nature spirits from another game.

The name Sakura is a real word and is already used by famous characters elsewhere. This Sakura has to stay our queen. She does not borrow those characters' lives, clothes, or fighting styles. A lawyer should look at the name before release.

| Queen | What people say | What their home would add | How she fights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Violet | The eldest. She speaks as if the five share one voice. | The others never gave her that right. She is afraid the family will split. | Patient. She waits until a sister, or the player, repeats a pattern. |
| Lumi | The bright peacemaker. | She keeps talks alive by hiding bad news. The peace is real and incomplete. | Light, fast, stops attacking if a bout turns cruel, then punishes the pause she herself created. |
| Lavender | The quiet rite-keeper. | The rites are older than Nathanael's claim, and she will not say whose they were. | Slow casts with a visible tell. She will not break her own rite to win. |
| Rosie | The warm host. Easy to underestimate. | She has already promised the same alliance to two enemies. | Welcomes, then closes distance. The friendliness is not a feint the first time. |
| Sakura | The sister who blooms and strikes first. | She left the gardens once, and the leaving was not a betrayal. Violet still calls it one. | Short, decisive rushes. She fades if the fight drags. |

### The wolf siblings

Lukka is queen of one wolf people. Killi is king of the wolves who oppose her. They are brother and sister. The war is a misunderstanding. Neither of them is the secret villain.

People say the two packs have always been enemies. What happened is a message, a hunt, or a night attack that each was told the other began. Both acted on that story. The person who started the lie is still unknown. Do not decide that person without approval.

Lukka fights like a queen who expects the pack to finish her opening. Killi fights like a king who refuses to be the first to leave a wound unanswered. If they ever learn the truth, the grudge can break. The deaths already caused do not vanish.

### Villains

The owner wants a kind of villain whose two stories agree. They kill because they want to. There is no hidden kindness and no later reveal that excuses them.

Proposed face for that kind: **Holt**. The arena says he kills for the pleasure of it. His home says the same thing. He can be cut from the roster. He should not be rewritten into a tragic hero.

One exception, also proposed: **Calder Voss**. The name is a placeholder.

People say Calder butchered a parley and enjoyed it. What happened is that he cut down the side that broke the peace, and the survivors lied about who drew first. Holt was there and kept killing after the lie was already winning. A later story can put Calder beside the people who condemned him. That is the turn from villain to hero. Holt does not get that turn.

### Noah

Proposed. An original champion who shares an old name. He is not the flood story, and he is not a joke about drinking.

People say he is a drunk who should not be in the ring. What happened is that he held the Salt Gate through a night attack, and the cup came so he could sleep afterward. The heroism is real. The drinking is still harm. It does not give him power, and it does not cancel the times he failed someone while drunk.

In a fight he is slow to commit and dangerous once he does. He can miss because he is unsteady. That miss is honest.

### Nathanael

Proposed. He calls himself lord of the Greek gods. The full note is in `GODS_AND_MYTHOLOGY.md`.

People say a god-king has entered the tournament to be worshipped. What a later story might show is that the gods under him do not all accept the title, and that champions were gathered without his order. He is not the organizer unless you say so.

He fights proud and talks too long. An early round can be won because he assumes a mortal will kneel.

He loves Tracea, and she loves him. Their paths have not met in the Nexus. Nothing in the current story joins them. He is not written as having found her.

### Norah

Proposed. A loving, sweet elf who cares for humans. Her two stories agree. The care is not a mask.

She is an original person. She does not borrow the looks, lineage, or manners of elves from other games or books.

People say she is too gentle for a tournament. What her home shows is the same gentleness put to work: she feeds, shelters, and stands with the humans who live beside her grove. She is not a queen. She is not secretly cruel. Other peoples are not her enemies. Humans are simply the ones she chose.

In a fight she steps between a falling person and the next hit. She hesitates to finish a downed human, and that pause is real. Against someone like Holt, who kills because he wants to, she does not offer that pause.

### Obidiah

Proposed. A grumpy dragon who is tired of people trespassing. The name is spelled the way you wrote it. His two stories agree. The temper is not a disguise.

He is not Vessa. She burned a road to save nests. He wants his own ground left alone. He is an original dragon, not one taken from another game.

People say he is a miser who hates visitors. What his home shows is the same dislike, with a reason you can see: paths, hunters, and champions keep cutting across land he has already marked. He is tired, not secretly kind, and not a killer for sport. Someone who turns around at the mark is allowed to leave.

In a fight he punishes the step that crosses his line. He does not chase a person who gets back out. The arena can include that line, so the grudge is visible.

### Tracea

Proposed. Queen of the elvish fairies, and the champion trying to save everyone from the Nexus. She is an unlocked boss in the first game. She does not replace the story bosses.

She is an original person. She does not borrow a fairy queen from another game or book. Her court is not the Five Gardens, and it is not Norah's grove. Norah is a different elf. Whether Norah belongs to Tracea's court is not decided.

People say she rules the fairies and all things mystical, and that she entered the tournament to lead the captives out. The fairy crown is hers. "All things mystical" is the size of her claim. Lavender's rites and Nathanael's gods are not automatically under her. Sporell's memory is not her spell.

What remains open is whether she can actually save anyone. She has been trying. She has not done it yet. She may be right that the Nexus is a trap. The first game does not confirm that from her mouth alone. She is not the organizer.

She loves Nathanael, and he loves her. She does not know he is part of the tournament. She can speak of him without knowing he is already here. The story does not bring their paths together yet. Loving him is not why she saves the player, and it does not make him the organizer.

The owner set this sequence on 2026-10-28. Tracea is not the first boss, and she is not the seventh. The seventh boss has no name yet. It is impossible to kill, and it kills the player. That loss is intentional. The 17th time it kills the player, Tracea comes in and saves them. The number 17 is the owner's choice. The attempt count lives in the local save.

She worries about the player and tries to leave. The player insists on going with her. She answers with a challenge: keep up. She wins that fight by the rules. She does not kill the player, and she does not die. She accepts them anyway. The acceptance is the success. Knocking her down is not the goal.

After she accepts them, she stays and grants powers across the rest of the journey. They are free adventure and casual unlocks, not a purchase, and not part of the competitive standard loadout. The exact powers are not chosen.

In her challenge she uses the same combat actions as every other boss. The magic has a visible tell. The seventh boss is the one that does not.

A minor voice in the first game, such as a fisher, a scout, or a child who is never shown, may be reserved as FUTURE. Write that reservation in this file before the voice is reused as a hero. Do not invent the promotion silently.

## The seven legendary characters

Supplied by the owner on 2026-10-31, with seven portrait cards (`NAH_Seven_Character_Portraits.zip`). The cards are mood references, not approved designs.

The owner keeps these seven names: **Tracea, Nathanael, Norah, Noah, Obi, Lukka, and Killi.** Their race and class names are separate, and everything below except the names is PROPOSED until the owner approves it. The cards still show Alluka and Killua; on 2026-10-31 the owner renamed them: Killua is Killi, and Alluka is Lukka.

Proposed role: Legendary Founders, story characters with their own bloodlines and signature classes. In multiplayer they get no unfair stat advantage. Their rarity comes from lore, looks, voice, animation, and playstyle. That matches the existing rule that competitive arena uses a standard loadout.

An earlier draft gave Tracea "the Everblossom" (Aelavari race, Virelisse class) and Norah "the Moon's Lullaby" (Lunavelle race, Velunara class, weaver of dreams). The owner then moved the moonlit look to Tracea and made Norah darker and woodsier. The table shows the newer version. Alternate names from the same note: for Tracea, races Aelunara, Selvariel, and Moonveil Elthari, and titles such as "Keeper of the Moonwild"; for Norah, races Gloamthorn, Noxwyn, and Duskwilder, classes Gloamweaver, Nightroot Warden, and Duskbriar Huntress, and titles such as "Keeper of the Hollow Grove".

How this meets the earlier proposals in this file:

- Tracea was proposed as queen of the elvish fairies. She is now also a moon-blessed elven goddess of blossoms and animals.
- Norah was proposed as a sweet elf who cares for the humans beside her grove. Her new look is dark and mysterious. Whether her personality changes too, or only her look, is not decided.
- Nathanael was proposed as a self-declared lord of the Greek gods. His new title makes him a star guardian.
- Noah held the Salt Gate, a port, so a tide-and-storm identity fits. His drinking and his heroism are unchanged unless the owner says otherwise.
- Lukka and Killi appear on the cards as spirit dogs. The owner mapped them onto the earlier pair, so they seem to be the same characters as the wolf siblings above. On 2026-10-31 the owner chose the spelling Lukka (earlier notes said Lluka), and every file now uses it. Whether they are still wolves at war or are now spirit dogs is not decided.
- Obi also appears as a spirit dog. Whether Obi replaces Obidiah (the grumpy dragon) or is a new character is not decided.

**Name conflict, resolved:** the cards used Killua and Alluka, the names of a brother and sister in the manga and anime *Hunter × Hunter* (Killua Zoldyck and Alluka Zoldyck). On 2026-10-31 the owner renamed them Killi and Lukka, so that pair is no longer used. Killi is close to Kíli, a dwarf in Tolkien's *The Hobbit*, a name Tolkien took from the Old Norse Poetic Edda. The risk is low, but it belongs in the lawyer's name review, with Sakura.

Still open: whether the other six personalities and powers come from real-life inspirations, like Tracea, or are fully fictional.

## MYSTERY

Why each champion was chosen. Whether a loser is freed, kept, or returned. Whether someone in the first roster already knows the organizer.

## FUTURE

A champion who loses, or who seems cruel, can be the lead of a later game set in their home. That game is where the second story can be walked through. It adds what the tournament omitted. It does not erase the believed story, and it does not contradict an approved fact.

## Consistency

If a proposed fact is promoted to CANON, note the date and the decision in this file. Do not change an approved fact in a sequel without that note.
