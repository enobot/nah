# Story timeline

Status: proposed. Dates are story order, not a calendar we have locked.

## Before the summons

1. Queen Solenne Marrow of Hearthglass signs a grain treaty. Wickmarket is left off the list. People in the tunnels go hungry.
2. Puckle Harn steals a ceremonial crown and sells the jewels for grain. He keeps the ugly frame and wears it. Hearthglass calls him the Scrap-King.
3. Solenne orders the Cinder Pass cleared so her army can move. Empress Vessa Cinderwing burns the pass instead, to stop that army reaching the nests. The nests live. The road dies. Solenne calls her a monster.
4. Bram Icehide, oath-guard of the White Shelf, freezes the only harbor rather than let Solenne's ships land. He saves his hold and traps his own fishers. He believes the oath was kept.
5. Sporell Quill, in Capwood, begins hearing old summons in the fungal net. Sporell tells nobody who matters. The net remembers earlier tournaments.
6. Mimsy Toll, fool in Solenne's hall, hears her say the words "if the lock needs a jailer, better one of ours." Mimsy repeats the line as a joke. Solenne banishes Mimsy to the Laughing Keep.
7. Each of them, and the player, is taken at a moment when they choose what honor means. They wake in the Nexus.

## During the tournament

Proposed chapter order. This matches the five adventure places already sketched in `GAME_DESIGN.md`, plus a sixth bout.

1. Wickmarket yard. Boss: Puckle Harn.
2. Capwood. Boss: Sporell Quill.
3. White Shelf. Boss: Bram Icehide.
4. Cinder Reach. Boss: Vessa Cinderwing.
5. Laughing Keep. Boss: Mimsy Toll.
6. The Clerk's circle. Boss: Solenne Marrow, then the question of the Keeper.

Arena mode can use these fighters as bots without playing the chapters.

## What is still unknown on purpose

- Whether the Clerk is a person, a spell, or the previous Keeper.
- Whether the lock holds back a disaster or only holds the champions.
- Whether any ending sends people to the right home.

Those stay unknown until you pick an ending direction.
