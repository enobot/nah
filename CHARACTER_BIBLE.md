# Character bible

Status: proposed, except the player rule below, which is the chosen way to fit custom champions into one story.

## The player

The Unwritten champion. Species, body, class, weapon, and clothes come from the creator. An optional motive picks dialogue, not a private plot.

Motives: survive, go home, take power, take revenge, protect someone, learn the truth.

The player has no fixed homeland. Other champions react to what they see and to the motive. They do not require a unique history for every combination.


Nobody in this set is only a villain or only a hero. Solenne wants her child safe and is willing to jail strangers to do it. Puckle is a thief and a provider. Vessa is a savior and an arsonist. Bram is loyal and cruel. Sporell is gentle and withholding. Mimsy is funny and the one who can ruin a queen with a true joke.

Full combat and story sheets for those six are in `BOSS_DESIGN.md`.

Holt is the villain whose stories match. Calder is the one who can become a hero when the second story is known. The flower queens and the wolf siblings are not secretly pure or secretly cruel. Noah is a hero and an alcoholic at the same time. Nathanael is a claim, not proof that he runs the Nexus. Norah's sweetness is the truth in both stories. Obidiah is grumpy because people will not stay off his ground. Tracea is trying to get everyone out. Wanting that does not mean she knows how, or that she is right about the Nexus. She loves Nathanael, and he loves her. She does not know he was gathered. The story does not join them yet. She is not the first boss. The seventh boss is an unnamed fight that cannot be won and always kills the player. On the 17th death she saves them, tries to leave, and then fights to see if they can keep up. She wins that fight and accepts them. She does not die. After that she keeps granting powers.
