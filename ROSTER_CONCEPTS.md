# Roster concepts

Status: PROPOSED. A concept library of 20 races and 18 classes supplied by the owner on 2026-10-31, with a design review and a suggested shortlist. Nothing here is approved: names, powers, looks, and lore wait for the owner's choice. No name has been checked for conflicts yet; that check must happen before commercial use.

Races give natural traits; classes decide how a fighter fights. Any race can take any class, which matches canon ("species do not lock classes"). The owner also wants familiar families (humans, elves, goblins, trolls, giants, bears, wolves, foxes, cats, dogs, rabbits, dragons, reptiles, birds, amphibians, insect-like species, undead, spirits, constructs, hybrids) as original N.A.H. versions. The placeholder roster already in the game (Human, Elf, Dwarf, Bear, and others, with 8 placeholder classes) stays until the owner picks.

## How the review was done

Each concept got three marks:

- **Distinctive:** how different it is from common fantasy races and classes.
- **Funny:** how surprising or silly it can get in a combination, since the owner wants chaos and comedy.
- **Prototype fit:** what it needs, compared with what exists today.
  - **Now:** fits the current systems: stats from body, weapon, race, and class; body-sized hitboxes; feet-to-head swings; Block with a shield that breaks and cools down; lock-on.
  - **Soon:** needs one system that is planned anyway: attack timing and combos, a dodge, pickups, or knockback.
  - **Later:** needs a large system: flight, heavy visual effects, arena objects, grapples, or a non-humanoid rig.

## Overlaps to settle

- Waystitcher and Rift Cartographer both reshape movement routes.
- Moonmolts and Dreamlings both make decoys.
- Threadborn and Nestwalkers both dodge at a cost.
- Woundgardener and Griefblooms both turn damage into healing.
- Chainwright and Starhook Hunter both pull.
- Lastlight Herald and the placeholder Wrathborn both grow stronger at low health.
- Lanternmaws and Thorn Surgeon both mark targets.

## Suggested shortlist for the first multiplayer prototype

Four races and four classes. Each one plays differently: movement, stance, defensive rhythm, and body shape for races; defense-to-offense, tempo, precision, and risk for classes. All of them fit today's systems, and all four races can use the humanoid animation family, which keeps animation cost down.

| Race | Special trait | Weakness | Counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kettleback | Steam burst: a quick dash in any direction | Overheats: no dash and slower movement for a few seconds | Bait the dash, punish the overheat |
| Thornwrought | Root: takes much less damage and cannot be moved | Cannot move or turn quickly while rooted; uprooting takes time | Circle behind, or wait them out |
| Glazeling | Deflect: the first hit after a calm moment is mostly turned aside | Cracks: every heavy hit in a short time makes the next one hurt more | Chain heavy hits; do not give them a breather |
| Paperkin | Fold narrow (smaller hitbox, faster) or broad (bigger shield coverage) | Narrow fold is fragile; changing shape takes a moment; burning later | Punish the fold change |

| Class | Combo identity | Counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tollkeeper | Block to fill a toll meter, then the next swing releases it as bonus damage | Stop feeding the shield, break it with heavy weapons, or wait for the toll to drain |
| Debtkeeper | Borrow a burst: faster swings and movement for a few seconds, then a slow recovery with no blocking | Block or back off through the burst, then punish the recovery |
| Thorn Surgeon | A hit marks the target; a follow-up on the mark within a short window deals bonus damage | The mark is visible and fades; block or retreat until then |
| Oathbearer | Swear one vow before a round for a strong benefit with a strict limit | The vow shows on screen; play around its limit |

Next after the shortlist: the Knellstriker, because its rhythm combos are the traditional button-combo system the game needs anyway, and then Echoforms and Tumblehorns.

## Funniest combinations

From the shortlist:

- **Paperkin + Tollkeeper:** a sheet of paper that soaks up a greataxe and folds the toll back into your face.
- **Thornwrought + Debtkeeper:** a rooted tree that borrows energy, goes feral for four seconds, then has to sit down and photosynthesize.
- **Glazeling + Oathbearer (Bare Blade):** a porcelain duelist who swore never to block. A glass cannon, literally.
- **Kettleback + Thorn Surgeon:** steam in, mark, vent, steam back for the finish.

The owner's own examples (Paperkin + Gravity Wrestler, Nestwalker + Wardforger, Cloudhollow + Chainwright, Candleheart + Ash Jester, Tumblehorn + Woundgardener, Bellwraith + Mirror Duelist) stay on the list for later, once their systems exist.

## Balance flags

- Echoforms + Knellstriker: an echo could repeat a finisher. A rule would be needed, for example that echoes cannot copy finishers.
- Cloudhollows: a flier above a feet-to-head swing could never be hit. An anti-air rule would be needed before flight.
- Mossbacks, Griefblooms, and the Woundgardener all heal in PvP. Healing needs firm limits.
- Every race and class still multiplies body, weapon, race, and class stats, so a very strong race with a very strong class needs a test before release.

## Future: hybrids and unlockable techniques

Owner's recommendation, 2026-10-31: later, add hybrid races and combat techniques unlocked by beating champions and bosses. That fits the existing rule that competitive arena uses a standard loadout, so experienced players gain choices, not raw power, in PvP.
