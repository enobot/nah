# Nexus mystery

## CANON

The champions are gathered and told to compete. They are not told who did it, why these people, or what winning and losing truly do.

The first game gives a satisfying ending for the people the player met. It does not have to explain the Nexus forever.

The organizer may be lying. The game should not decide that until the owner approves an explanation.

## MYSTERY

Leave these open unless a decision in this file promotes an answer to CANON:

- Who gathers the champions?
- Why these champions?
- Has the tournament happened before?
- Are the realms one world or many?
- What happens to someone who is finally defeated?
- Is the public prize the truth?
- Are any gods involved, or only believers?
- What does honor mean here, if the organizer is using the word?
- Were they gathered to find the strongest, or for some other purpose?

One question stays reserved for the franchise, even if the first game ends well: the champions may not have been gathered to decide who is strongest. They may have been gathered to prepare people whose separate worlds could not survive something alone. The owner asked to keep that idea on 2026-10-28. It is not canon. The first game must not confirm it.

## PROPOSED interpretations

These are rival ideas. They are not the plot.

1. **The lock.** The Nexus holds something shut. The winner stays as keeper. Home is the lie. This is the version sketched in `WORLD_BIBLE.md`.
2. **The test.** Someone is choosing a ruler, a guardian, or a weapon, and the fights are the interview.
3. **The debt.** The champions are pulled because their homes owe a price, and the tournament decides who pays.
4. **The mistake.** The gathering was an old spell that no longer has a master. The Clerk recites rules nobody enforces.
5. **The preparation.** The tournament is not a search for the strongest. It is practice for a danger no single realm could face alone. This is the reserved franchise idea above. It can sit beside the lock, the test, the debt, or the mistake. It is not the plot until the owner says so.

The player can hear clues that fit more than one of these. Sporell's memory and Mimsy's joke are useful only if those characters are approved. Until then, the arenas should still be able to hint without naming them.

## Endings

`BOSS_DESIGN.md` suggests four endings. They are PROPOSED. A good first-game ending answers what this champion chooses. It does not need to say which interpretation is the real history of the Nexus.

## FUTURE

Later games can confirm one interpretation, or show that two were true at once. That confirmation is a creative decision recorded here before the script says it out loud.
