# Art direction

Approved visual, animation, audio, and lore choices go here. A placeholder is not an approved design.

## Approved

Recorded 2026-10-31.

- The first fighter moves slowly, stays planted, and feels very heavy. This pass is local only.
- The temporary keys may be used: WASD to move, mouse to look, Space to jump, Shift to sprint, left click to attack, and E to interact. Gamepad buttons in the same prototype file are available too. This is not the final layout.

Recorded 2026-10-31, the owner's direction for two of the legendary characters. These set a direction; they are not final models.

- **Tracea:** a moon-blessed spring goddess and elf. Elegant, soft, celestial, and loving, with animals that trust her instantly. She looks soft but is ancient and powerful. Themes: moonlight, cherry blossoms, animals, gentle divinity. Palette: silvery moonlight, lavender, dusty rose, pale pink, soft plum, pearl white, and a cool blue glow.
- **Norah:** a dark woodland dusk spirit. Beautiful but unsettling, quiet, wise, and hidden. Themes: deep and night forest, moss, thorns, bark, shadows, hidden paths. Palette: deep forest green, blackened emerald, dark brown, muted moss, smoky charcoal, shadowy violet, and a faint amber or dim green glow.

## Mood references

- Seven portrait cards for Tracea, Nathanael, Norah, Noah, Obi, Lukka, and Killi (the cards still say Alluka and Killua, the names before the owner's rename), supplied by the owner on 2026-10-31 (`NAH_Seven_Character_Portraits.zip`, and the same cards shown in chat). They are mood references only, not approved designs. They are not imported into the game.
- A Tracea reference sheet supplied the same day: eight expressions (gentle smile, happy, laugh, surprised, wink, thoughtful, determined, serene), a front, side, and back turnaround, close-up details, and scenes with deer, foxes, wolves, and rabbits. Useful for modeling later. Same status as the cards.
- A second, more detailed Tracea sheet, the same day. It lists her themes as moon, blossom, animals, nature, and divinity, and shows her race and class as Lunarethiel and Seleneva. New ideas on it: a crescent-moon staff with a glowing orb, a glowing moon bow, riding a giant white wolf, floating over a magic circle, a flower tiara with a center gem, crescent earrings, and jewelry down the back of the dress. It also has a front, 3/4, side, and back turnaround. Same status as the cards: these are ideas to choose from, not approved designs or gameplay. The "N.A.H." logo on the cards is not approved branding. Who or what tool made the images is not recorded; see `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md`.

## Open decisions

- How the first fighter should look: species, colors, proportions, clothes, and weapon.
- How Nathanael, Noah, Obi, Lukka, and Killi should look. Their cards are mood references only.
- How attacks should look and how long startup, hit, and recovery should feel.
- The first arena: place, colors, light, and atmosphere.
- Whether the temporary keys stay after this local test.
- The lock-on button. It uses temporary keys added on 2026-10-31 (middle click or Q, right-stick click on a gamepad). They are not approved.
- The Block button. It uses temporary keys added on 2026-10-31 (right click, left bumper on a gamepad). They are not approved. How a block and a breaking shield look and sound is not decided.
