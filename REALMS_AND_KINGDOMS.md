# Realms and kingdoms

## CANON

More than one homeland exists. Champions did not all come from the same court. The Nexus is the meeting place, not their birthplace.

A realm in the story is only a name, a culture, and a reason a champion fights. It is not a game map.

## PROPOSED

`FACTIONS_AND_KINGDOMS.md` suggests Wickmarket, Capwood, the White Shelf, Hearthglass, Cinder Reach, and the Laughing Keep. Those names are not approved.

Each proposed place argues about a different meaning of honor. People of many species can live in one place. A species is not a nation.

## MYSTERY

Whether the realms are separate worlds, far countries, or something the Nexus only makes look separate.

## FUTURE

If a proposed realm is approved and the first game is done, that realm could later be its own story: settlements, quests, and choices played where the first game only talked about them.

Do not build that travel, map, or quest system into Nexus of Ancient Honor.

## First-game rule

The adventure visits arena versions of a few places. Those visits teach combat. They are not open worlds. A banner, a line of dialogue, and a reward can suggest a culture. A second game would be where the player walks the real streets.
