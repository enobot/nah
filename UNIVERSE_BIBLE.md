# Universe bible

Labels used across the franchise notes:

- CANON: a creative decision the owner has set for this project. The title is still not a cleared trademark.
- PROPOSED: an idea waiting for approval. The game will not state it as fact.
- MYSTERY: a question the story is supposed to leave open for now.
- FUTURE: a possible later story. It is not a feature of the first game.

## CANON

The first game is **N.A.H. — Nexus of Ancient Honor**. The letters stay. H is read as Honor. The working line is "Honor is not given. It is earned."

The first game introduces one shared fantasy universe. Champions from different species, civilizations, and beliefs are gathered in the Nexus and told to compete. They do not know who gathered them, why they were chosen, or what the tournament accomplishes.

The first game establishes that mystery. It does not answer every question. It still needs a complete match, boss arcs that pay off inside this game, and a conclusion that is worth reaching.

Arena play can ignore the story. The story does not change combat rules, unlock pricing, or multiplayer authority.

Species do not lock classes. A custom player is the Unwritten champion. An optional motive can change lines. It does not require a separate plot for every body and class.

Every major champion has two stories: what the tournament says, and what their own world would show. Those accounts may agree or diverge. A divergence is not a secret morality flip. See `CHAMPION_LORE.md`.

## PROPOSED

Named realms, gods, and the six bosses in `BOSS_DESIGN.md` are proposals. So is any single explanation of the Nexus, including the idea that it is a lock and the winner becomes its keeper.

## MYSTERY

Who gathered the champions. Why these people. Whether the tournament has happened before. What happens to someone who loses for good. Whether the organizer is telling the truth. How the realms are connected. Whether any god is more than a local belief.

Also reserved, and not canon: the tournament might be preparation for a danger no single realm could survive alone. The first game does not answer that. See `NEXUS_MYSTERY.md`.

## FUTURE

A later game could visit one champion's home and play events that the first game only describes. A minor speaker in the first game could lead that later story. An apparent villain could be shown from the other side.

Those visits are not built now. The first game does not gain an open world, a quest log, or sequel-only systems in order to make them possible.

## What the first game must not borrow

No characters, gods, names, or tournament rules from other franchises. A web search found other uses of the word Nexus, including another fictional tournament of warriors from many worlds. Our places, clerks, and champions stay original.

No domain, logo, or public brand is approved. A lawyer should review the title before release.
