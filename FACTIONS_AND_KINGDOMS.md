# Factions and kingdoms

Status: proposed. These places are not approved names.

Species do not belong to only one place. A duck can be from a treaty city. A bear can leave the ice. A class is still a choice, not a birthplace.

| Place | Who lives there | What they argue about | Story job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wickmarket | Goblins and anyone small enough to use the tunnels | Whether a crown is theft or protection | First arena. Teaches movement and a straight fight. |
| Capwood | People who tend giant fungi, including small beasts and wanderers | Whether memory in the soil is a person | Ranged and magical enemies. A boss who already remembers other summons. |
| The White Shelf | Bears, foxes, and other winter peoples, plus their guests | Whether an oath outranks a life | Ice hazards. A boss who fights by a written code. |
| Hearthglass | A treaty city of many species, ruled in public by a queen | Whether order is the same as honor | The queen's past touches the other champions. |
| Cinder Reach | Dragons and the people of the pass below the aeries | Whether burning a road is a crime or a shield | Flying enemies and fire. A boss called a monster for a choice she does not regret. |
| The Laughing Keep | Exiles, fools, and people the other courts threw out | Whether a joke can be truer than a law | Trick fights. The jester heard a secret. |
| The Nexus | No one is born here | Whether the Clerk's rules are honor or a cage | The tournament itself. |

Hearthglass is the political center of the proposed timeline. It is not a required playable level in the first adventure slice. The queen enters as the sixth boss, after the Keep.

No flags, crests, or god names from other games belong to these places.
