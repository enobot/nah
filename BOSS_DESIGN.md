# Boss design

Status: proposed. These six fill the adventure roles already listed in `GAME_DESIGN.md`. Names are not canon. Combat numbers are not chosen. No dialogue is final.

Each boss uses the shared combat actions. Personality changes which actions they prefer. It does not add a private damage system.

`CHAMPION_LORE.md` gives each of these six a public story and a home story. The fight should play the public story. A line, a note, or a reward can crack it. The rest of the home story can wait for optional scenes in this game, or for a later visit.

The owner also put these champions in the first game as unlocked bosses. They are not in the story ladder above. Their sheets are still the short notes in `CHAMPION_LORE.md`.

- Violet, Lumi, Lavender, Rosie, Sakura
- Lukka, Killi
- Holt, Calder Voss
- Noah, Nathanael, Norah, Obi (replaced Obidiah on 2026-10-31), Tracea

An unlocked boss uses the same combat actions as every other boss. The fight stays locked until the story path opens it. No separate damage system.

Tracea is not boss 1 and not boss 7. Boss 7 is unnamed. It cannot be killed. Each of its kills is one try, stored in the local save. On try 17, Tracea saves the player. She tries to leave. The player insists on coming. Her own fight is boss 8. She wins it by the rules, does not kill the player, and accepts them. She stays alive and grants powers across the rest of the journey. Those powers are free adventure unlocks. They do not enter the competitive standard loadout. What each power does is not chosen yet.

Rewards mentioned in `GAME_DESIGN.md` stay attached to the same roles. Gameplay rewards stay out of the competitive standard loadout.

| Title | Scrap-King |
| Species | Goblin |
| Homeland | Wickmarket |
| Personality | Loud, reckless, proud of a crooked crown |

| Secret | The jewels are already gone. The crown is a receipt, not wealth |
| Reward | The oversized goblin crown, cosmetic |
| Consequence | Wickmarket hears that their king is in the Nexus |
| Connection | Solenne's treaty is why he stole |

## Endings

Proposed. Pick none until you say so.

1. **The Keeper.** The player takes the job. The lock holds. The others stay. The child is unnamed and unvisited. A heavy win.
2. **The cracked ring.** The player and at least one boss refuse the last blow. Gates open onto mismatched homes. Some people are lost. The lock's fate is unknown.
3. **The spoken sentence.** The player gathers the notes and says Mimsy's line in the circle. The champions vote. The Clerk must answer with a rule that is no longer a secret.
4. **The changed law.** The player becomes Keeper and rewrites who gets pulled. This can protect or punish. It is the dangerous win.

All four are defeatable fights before the choice. The choice is not a surprise kill.

These names, the Keeper mystery, the six histories, and the endings are proposals. Say which to keep, rename, or throw out. Until then they are not lore the game will state as fact.
