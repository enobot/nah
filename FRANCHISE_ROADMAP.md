# Franchise roadmap

## CANON priority

Nexus of Ancient Honor is the game being built. A later installment is not a reason to weaken it.

Build, in this order:

1. Fluid combat.
2. Character customization.
3. Intelligent enemies.
4. Memorable bosses.
5. Single-player progression.
6. Multiplayer.
7. Environmental detail.
8. Interconnected storytelling.
9. Free unlockable rewards.
10. Performance, security, and stability.

Story documents do not add open-world code, quest systems, or sequel save formats to the first project.

## This game

One tournament. A small set of arenas. Custom champions. Bots and bosses who share player combat actions. Local saves. No cloud, no paid rewards, and no unapproved assets.

The story introduces the mystery and ends this champion's tournament. See `NEXUS_MYSTERY.md`.

## FUTURE, not scheduled

After this game is actually playable and the owner still wants more:

- One approved realm could become its own story, played where the arena only mentioned it.
- A minor character could become a lead.
- A past event could be shown from the other side.
- One proposed explanation of the Nexus could be confirmed.
- The reserved question in `NEXUS_MYSTERY.md` could be answered: whether the tournament was practice for a danger no single realm could face. It stays unanswered in the first game.

Each of those needs its own approval. None of them has a build plan, a budget, or a date.

## Legal

The title is the creative name of the project. It has not been cleared. No domain or public brand is approved. Before a store page, a qualified attorney should review the name, the major characters, and `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md`.
